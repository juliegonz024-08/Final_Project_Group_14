Project Proposal Plan Group 14
Project Abstract 
Our Project is focusing on Education in Cuba and using the dataset from data.cenus.gov we can evaluate the data given and process the data into visual graphics to understand where the peaks and drops are in education. The goal is to raise the education scores in Cuba and help solve the overall issue of drought. We choose this dataset since it gives a clear overview of the percentage scores in Cuba and individual datasets for each subcategory such as graduates from college and their degree types in addition to high school and college enrollment. Evaluating and analyzing the dataset our group will attain the problem and create a solution to the low scores and connect it to the drought in Cuba. Our motivation is to increase the education scores and get more retainment from the schools there in hopes to bring knowledge and education to the area. As the scores and retainment improve we can become knowledgeable in the water shortage and create solutions to create a successful area.
